OUR MISSION • CURATING PAN-AFRICAN EXCELLENCE

Africa is building. We help the world see it.

Discova is the premier editorial directory and community dedicated to reviewing, upvoting, and amplifying the highest-caliber software, AI models, and infrastructure emerging across the continent.

“The talent in Lagos, Nairobi, and Kigali is building for the next billion users — not just cloning Western apps.”
THE ORIGIN STORY

Why we started Discova

For years, African technology narratives were trapped in narrow tropes: either sensationalized poverty alleviation or copy-paste replicas of Silicon Valley giants. But on the ground, builders were solving radically harder engineering problems.

Founders were creating zero-fee micro-savings powered by stablecoins to hedge against double-digit inflation. Engineers were training large language models on Pidgin and Yoruba to enable conversational commerce for unbanked shopkeepers. Infrastructure architects were deploying solar-powered satellite mesh nodes across schools with zero grid power.

We built Discova to provide the definitive global stage these products deserve. No hype cycles, no pay-to-play rankings — just rigorous product curation, peer upvotes, and unfiltered founder stories told in their own voices.

CORE PHILOSOPHY

What we believe

The foundational tenets that guide how we curate, rank, and celebrate African technology.

01

Curated, not crowded

We don't publish everything that applies. Every product undergoes technical verification, functional testing, and security checks before appearing in the directory.

02

Builders first

Our platform is designed for the engineers and founders in the trenches. Feedback is actionable, analytics are transparent, and community upvotes are organic.

03

No clone framing

We celebrate products designed for local market dynamics and global scale — never dismissing African innovation as mere 'Uber for X' or 'Stripe for Y'.

04

The whole continent

From Francophone West Africa to North African hubs and Southern tech clusters, we track innovation across all 54 nations, dismantling regional silos.
